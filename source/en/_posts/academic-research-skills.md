## Overview

**Academic Research Skills (ARS)** is a professional academic research toolkit for Claude Code, packaging the complete research workflow — from literature survey to paper publication — into orchestratable AI skill pipelines.

{% note success %}
A "tool for tools" — helping AI better assist researchers, not replace them.
{% endnote %}

## Four Skill Packages

### Deep Research

13 specialized agents in a team, 7 working modes:

| Mode | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| Full Research | End-to-end academic study |
| Quick Brief | Rapid literature overview |
| Lit Review | Systematic literature review |
| Systematic Review | PRISMA protocol + meta-analysis |
| Fact Check | Claim accuracy verification |
| Socratic Guide | Dialogue-based research mentoring |
| Paper Review | Pre-submission manuscript review |

### Academic Paper

12 agents in a writing pipeline, 10 modes:

- Full paper / outline / abstract / lit review generation
- Revision & revision coaching
- Citation format checking
- Format conversion (LaTeX / DOCX / PDF)
- Bilingual abstracts (Chinese/English)

{% label APA 7.0 blue %} {% label IEEE green %} {% label MLA pink %} {% label Chicago orange %} {% label Vancouver red %}

### Academic Paper Reviewer

5 independent reviewers for multi-perspective evaluation:

- **EIC**: overall assessment and verdict
- **3 peer reviewers**: methodology, theory, experimental design
- **Devil's Advocate**: finds the toughest issues

Each reviewer has domain-specific expertise and a 0-100 scoring rubric.

### Academic Pipeline

10-stage end-to-end workflow:

```
Research → Write → Integrity Check → Review → Revise → Re-review → Re-revise → Final Check → Finalize
```

Each stage has a **mandatory quality gate**.

## Key Innovations

### Anti-Hallucination Verification

Mandatory claim-to-source cross-checking ensures every AI-generated statement is traceable.

### Style Calibration

Automatically adapts output to target journal/conference writing style.

### Socratic Mentoring

Guides researchers by asking questions rather than giving answers directly.

## Why This Matters

The motivation is simple: academic writing demands extreme rigor, and AI hallucination is the biggest obstacle. ARS's core philosophy is **fighting hallucination with process and verification** — not letting AI write for you, but making AI a reliable academic collaborator.
